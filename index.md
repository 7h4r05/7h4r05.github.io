# Welcome to My Blog!

Welcome to my blog, where I share my thoughts, experiences, and practical insights on AI, cloud computing, and software architecture.

This is my fourth attempt at blogging consistently, so let's hope it sticks this time! ;)

I'm mainly interested in **AI, cloud, and software architecture**, with a strong focus on the **Microsoft and Azure ecosystem**, but I'm not limiting myself to it.

Some of the topics I'll be writing about:

- **AI & Generative AI** — LLMs, AI agents, and real-world use cases.
- **RAG & AI Architecture** — embeddings, vector search, and working with custom data.
- **Cloud Architecture** — designing scalable, secure, and reliable solutions.
- **Microsoft Azure** — Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search, and cloud-based AI applications.
- **DevOps & Engineering** — CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, and automation.

I want to focus on practical things: experimenting with new technologies, sharing what works (and what doesn't), and exploring different approaches to solving real-world problems.

Let's see where this blog goes. Hopefully, there will be plenty of things to learn and share along the way!
